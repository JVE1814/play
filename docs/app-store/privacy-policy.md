# Privacy Policy: Between Stops

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

Between Stops is a collection of offline games for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to
your information when you use it. In short: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## What we collect

Nothing. Between Stops does not collect, store on a server, transmit, sell or share any personal
data or usage data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no
tracking of any kind, and it contains no third-party code that could do so. The app does not use
the internet at all.

## What stays on your phone

To let you resume a game and see your progress, the app keeps a few things in its own private
storage on your device:

- the game you are playing (the position of the cards and your undo history),
- your statistics (games played and won, streaks, best time),
- your settings (sound, haptics, appearance, draw mode, left-handed layout).

This data never leaves your device, except as part of your own iCloud or computer backups of your
phone, which Apple and you control. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also clear your
statistics from the Statistics screen.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
Because the app collects nothing, a change would only follow a change to the app itself.
