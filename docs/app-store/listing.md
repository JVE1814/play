## Description (4000)

Between Stops is a small collection of calm, colorful games for the moments between here and there. The first is Solitaire: classic Klondike, rebuilt for one-handed play on a moving train.

MADE FOR ONE THUMB
• Tap any card and it makes its best move. No precise dragging needed (but you can drag too).
• The stock and the buttons sit at the bottom, within reach of your thumb. Left-handed? Flip it in Settings.
• Big cards and big buttons, forgiving of a bumpy ride.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
• Every move is saved instantly. Close the app, lock your phone, or force quit at your stop: the same game will be waiting, down to the last card.
• Unlimited undo, all the way back to the deal.

CLASSIC KLONDIKE
• Draw 1 or Draw 3, with unlimited redeals.
• Hints when you're stuck, and a one-tap finish once every card is face up.
• Moves and a timer for each game.
• Statistics: games won, win rate, best time, and current and best streaks.

CALM AND CLEAR
• Flat, bold colors in light and dark mode.
• Four distinct suit shapes, so you never have to rely on color.
• VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion support.
• Sound is off unless you want it, and the light haptics can be turned off.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works completely offline.
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Nothing leaves your phone.

## App Review notes

Between Stops is fully offline and has no accounts, sign-in, purchases or network access. To
try it: open Solitaire from the home screen, then tap any face-up card to move it, or tap the
stock (bottom right) to draw. Settings (gear icon) switches between Draw 1 and Draw 3.
