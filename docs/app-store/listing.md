## Description (4000)

Between Stops is a small collection of calm, colorful games for the moments between here and there. It has four games, all built for one-handed play on a moving train: Solitaire (classic Klondike), Bubbles, a bubble shooter with 2,000 levels, Archery, with 30 levels of target archery, and Runner, an endless run down a pier.

MADE FOR ONE THUMB
• Tap any card and it makes its best move. No precise dragging needed (but you can drag too).
• The stock and the buttons sit at the bottom, within reach of your thumb. Left-handed? Flip it in Settings.
• Big cards and big buttons, forgiving of a bumpy ride.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
• Every move is saved instantly. Close the app, lock your phone, or force quit at your stop: the same game will be waiting, down to the last card.
• Unlimited undo, all the way back to the deal.

BUBBLES: 2,000 LEVELS
• Aim with your thumb: point where to shoot, or pull back like a slingshot. The guide shows exactly where the bubble will land, bounces included.
• Match three to pop them, and cut bubbles loose to drop whole clusters.
• Stones, bombs and new shapes keep arriving as you go, with a gentler level every so often.
• Every level has been cleared by our own test player, so a solution always exists. Earn up to three stars on each.

ARCHERY: 30 LEVELS, 10 TO 90 METRES
• Touch and hold to draw the bow, slide your thumb to move the sight, and let go to shoot.
• Read the wind from the flag on the target and allow for it. Gusts come and go, and on some days the wind swings from side to side.
• Hold steady: the sight settles after you draw, then wanders as your arms tire.
• Five venues from a calm morning practice field to a windy championship under floodlights, with moving and swinging targets, small three-spot faces and races against the clock.
• A target camera shows your group, and every arrow scores just like real archery, X ring included.

RUNNER: HOW FAR CAN YOU GET?
• Swipe left or right to change lane, up to jump, down to slide.
• Jump the barriers and the rolling barrels, slide under the beams, go round the crates and containers.
• Coins along the way, a magnet that pulls them in, and a shield that takes one hit.
• It gets faster the further you run. Every run has a goal to beat, and your first runs show you the moves.
• Sunset in light mode, a moonlit night in dark mode.

CLASSIC KLONDIKE
• Draw 1 or Draw 3, with unlimited redeals.
• Hints when you're stuck, and a one-tap finish once every card is face up.
• Moves and a timer for each game.
• Statistics: games won, win rate, best time, and current and best streaks.

CALM AND CLEAR
• Flat, bold colors in light and dark mode.
• Four distinct suit shapes, so you never have to rely on color.
• VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion support.
• Sound is off unless you want it, and the light haptics can be turned off.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works completely offline.
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Nothing leaves your phone.

## App Review notes

Between Stops is fully offline and has no accounts, sign-in, purchases or network access. To
try it: open Solitaire from the home screen, then tap any face-up card to move it, or tap the
stock (bottom right) to draw. Settings (gear icon) switches between Draw 1 and Draw 3. In
Archery, touch and hold anywhere on the range to draw the bow, slide to aim, and let go to shoot.
In Runner, tap the pier to start, then swipe left, right, up or down.
